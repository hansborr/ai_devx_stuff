# Musi UX/UI Audit — Live Play Session (2026-06-06)

## Method

Three agents played a real D&D 5.5E session through three concurrent isolated browsers
against a local dev stack (client :8000, server :8001, seeded DB):

- **DM** (`dm@example.com`) — created campaign "The Sunken Crypt of Velgaroth", notes,
  NPCs, a 30×30 battle map, and the encounter "The Flooded Entry Hall" (2 Goblin
  Warriors + 2 Skeletons); ran 3 full combat rounds to resolution (both PCs downed,
  death saves, NPC rescue, XP summary).
- **Player 1 / Aragorn** — built "Strider" (Lv-1 Human Fighter, Soldier) through the
  full creation wizard; fought all 3 rounds (nat-1 miss, spear hit + Second Wind,
  death-save success).
- **Player 2 / Gandalf** — built "Mithrandir" (Lv-1 Human Wizard, Sage); cast Fire Bolt
  and Magic Missile (first kill), spent spell slots, tested rests, and replayed a turn
  at a 390×844 mobile viewport.

Rules of engagement: black-box only — discover everything through the UI, no reading
app source; log findings continuously with severity, expected-vs-actual, and
screenshots; log what works well too. Findings that turned out to be artifacts of the
*test harness* (queued inter-agent messages arriving late) were explicitly withdrawn
and are excluded below — the agents were disciplined about separating app defects from
coordination lag.

Raw logs: `/tmp/musi-ux-audit/{dm,player1,player2}-findings.md` (61 screenshots in
`/tmp/musi-ux-audit/screenshots/`). Severity forks left open by the players (e.g. "is
this server-enforced?") were settled post-session by code inspection; those
verifications are marked **[verified in code]** with file references.

## Executive summary

**The single-player surfaces are genuinely strong.** Character creation, sheet rules
math, spell-slot mechanics, the dice engine, chat, and the battle-map tooling all
exceeded expectations (details in "What works well"). **The serious problems
concentrate in two places: (1) spellcaster onboarding is broken at creation, and
(2) multiplayer combat state is not reliably shared** — the turn pointer, roster
state, and PC hit points can diverge between DM and player clients to the point where,
mid-session, the DM and a player held contradictory views of whose turn it was, what
round it was, and whether a character was conscious. A VTT lives or dies on the shared
board; today it requires manual refreshes and out-of-band narration to stay coherent.

## P0 — Fix before any real multiplayer session

### 1. Spellcasters are created with zero spells and no way to know it
*Witness: Gandalf (blocker). Screenshots: `gandalf-sheet-no-spells.png`, `gandalf-review-no-spells.png`*

The 8-step creation wizard has **no spell/cantrip selection step**. The Class step's own
description text promises "three Wizard cantrips of your choice" and "six level 1
Wizard spells", but no step ever asks. A finished Lv-1 wizard lands on a sheet reading
"No spells known — Add your first spell!" with Save DC/attack correctly computed and
nothing to cast. A new player has a caster that cannot cast and no signal that they
were supposed to do something.

**Fix direction:** add a spell-selection wizard step for caster classes (auto-grant
cantrip count at minimum), or auto-populate class cantrips + a default spellbook and
let players edit on the sheet. The post-hoc "+ Add" spell dialog is good — it's the
*mandatory discovery* of it that's broken.

### 2. Turn/round pointer does not push live → clients disagree on game state
*Witness: Aragorn F20 (high→borders blocker), corroborated by DM. Both directly observed.*

When the DM advances the turn, the "Turn X of N" counter updates live on player
clients, but the **"Current" combatant highlight does not move** until a hard refresh.
Late in the session this compounded into full state divergence: the DM's client was
stuck on "Round 1, Strider current" while the server (and a freshly reloaded player
client) was at "Round 2, Mithrandir current". Consequence chain observed live: the DM
prompted a player to act out of turn, then adjudicated damage against stale HP and
downed a PC who (per server state after a legitimate heal) should have survived at
3/12. The encounter *status* transitions (setup→active→resolved), chat, dice rolls,
presence, and damage-HP all broadcast fine — the gap is specific to the
turn/round-pointer (and roster, see P1-4) state.

**Fix direction:** broadcast turn-advance (and round-increment) over the same socket
path chat/HP already use, and/or invalidate the encounter query on `encounter:updated`.
Given partial updates *do* arrive (turn counter) while siblings don't (current
highlight), audit which fields of the encounter payload the client cache actually
refreshes. See `docs/socket-architecture.md` conventions.

### 3. PC hit points: sheet and encounter share one value with no event surfacing
*Witness: DM (high) + Aragorn F24 (high), root-caused jointly in play; **[verified in code]***

For character participants, the encounter reads HP/AC **directly from the
character-sheet stats row** — `resolveParticipantStats` returns `stats.currentHp` /
`stats.ac` (`packages/server/src/utils/encounter-query.ts:109-126`); the participant
row stores no combat snapshot (`packages/server/src/utils/encounter-participant-helpers.ts:19-21`).
That is a defensible single-source-of-truth design, **but nothing surfaces sheet-side
writes in the encounter context**: when Aragorn used Second Wind via his sheet's Heal
control (3→12), the DM saw tracked combat damage "silently revert to full" with no
log entry, no toast, no attribution — indistinguishable from data corruption. Combined
with P0-2's stale views, DM damage and player heals raced and the observed HP
"oscillated" (12→0→0 across reads). The DM's intermittent "first damage zeroed a
12/12 PC" report (observed once, not reproduced) is plausibly the same race seen from
the other side.

**Fix direction (decide one):**
- (a) Snapshot combat HP onto the participant at add-time and make the encounter
  tracker authoritative during active combat (sheet shows mirrored read-only value), or
- (b) keep the shared value but route *all* HP mutations through one mutation that
  emits a combat-log entry + broadcast with actor attribution ("Aragorn healed 9
  (Second Wind)"), so concurrent writers see each other.

Either way, `docs/CONCURRENCY.md` patterns apply — this is a two-writer race on a
race-sensitive field that already has `statsVersion` plumbing available.

## P1 — High: undermines trust or blocks a core flow

### 4. Character AC in encounters is wrong for every PC
*Witness: Aragorn F18 + DM (high) + Gandalf — all three seats; **[verified in code]***

Encounter cards showed AC 10 for both PCs; true ACs were 12 (10+DEX 2) and 11
(10+DEX 1). Monsters' AC is correct. Root cause: the sheet computes AC live via the
shared rules engine (`packages/shared/src/rules/character-rules.ts` — armor table,
`ArmorClassInput`), while the encounter displays the *persisted* `CharacterStats.ac`,
which stays at base 10. Every to-hit adjudication against a PC is wrong if the DM
trusts the card (≈+10% hit chance per understated point). Fix: persist computed AC on
stat-affecting changes, or derive AC at read time from the same rules engine the sheet
uses.

### 5. Rest modal never closes after confirming — invisibly blocks the whole sheet
*Witness: Gandalf (high), root-caused via DOM.*

After "Take Long Rest"/"Take Short Rest", the rest applies correctly, but the dialog
stays `data-state="open"` and its overlay **intercepts every subsequent click on the
sheet** with no visual indication why. Only Escape recovers. To a player this is "the
app stopped responding after I rested". Repro: open Long Rest → confirm → click any
sheet control. Fix: close the dialog on confirm success.

### 6. The player has no action surface on their own turn
*Witness: Gandalf (high); Aragorn (observation). Screenshot: `gandalf-my-turn.png`*

When the initiative tracker says it's your turn, the encounter view offers no Attack /
Cast / Roll / End-Turn affordance — clicking your combatant only selects the map
token. A player's turn is executed across three disconnected surfaces: roll in the
chat dice roller, spend the slot on the character sheet, then *tell the DM in prose*
so they can apply damage and advance the turn. It works (the DM-drives-the-board model
is legitimate), but rolls carry no character/spell/target context, so the system can
never tell hit from miss or apply damage. Related: the **Combat Log stayed empty all
session** (Gandalf, medium) — attacks, casts, and HP changes generate no entries, so
there is no audit trail of the fight. An on-turn action bar (or even just wiring
sheet-initiated attack/cast rolls into the combat log with target pickers) would
collapse the three-surface dance.

### 7. Casting a spell gives zero feedback
*Witness: Gandalf (high cantrips + medium leveled); pairs with Aragorn F11.*

Clicking **Cast** on Fire Bolt produces nothing — no roll, no toast, no log entry —
in stark contrast to ability/skill rolls, which toast nicely. Leveled casts decrement
the slot (visible) but the actual spell effect/roll is silent. Same family as Aragorn
F11: on a **standalone** sheet (no campaign context), *all* roll buttons appear dead —
they broadcast to a campaign log that doesn't exist there. In-campaign, sheet rolls
work great (labeled "Attack (Spear)" + auto damage roll). Fix: local result toast for
any roll/cast lacking a log surface; attach attack+damage rolls to Cast.

### 8. Character attach/assignment doesn't broadcast to other clients
*Witness: DM (medium) + Gandalf (high) — confirmed from both seats.*

Presence (online dots) updates live, but a player attaching their character does not
appear on anyone else's open Members tab/roster until manual refresh. This is the gap
that actually stalled the session: the DM sat "waiting" on players who had already
attached. Fix: broadcast member/character-assignment changes like presence already is.

### 9. Systemic accessibility gaps
*Witness: all three agents independently.*

- Every Radix dialog logs `Missing 'Description' or aria-describedby` (New Note, New
  NPC, Add Item, Add Spell, spell detail…). The Join Campaign dialog *has* one, so
  this is an oversight pattern, not a missing capability. Sweep `DialogContent`
  usages and add `DialogDescription`.
- Proficiency/language/skill toggle chips convey selected state **only via color** —
  `aria-pressed` is absent (Gandalf, DOM-verified). Screen-reader users get no signal;
  doubles as a colorblind issue. Likely recurs wherever chip multi-selects exist.

## What works well (keep and protect)

- **Combat console** (DM-side): initiative auto-roll + sort, Round/Turn-of-N tracker
  with wrap, color-coded HP bars, death-saves UI auto-surfacing at 0 HP, action-economy
  pips, **all 15 5E conditions with inline rules text**, encounter difficulty/XP
  auto-calc, polished End-Encounter XP summary, clean Setup→Active→Resolved lifecycle.
- **Dice engine**: freeform notation incl. `4d6kh3` with dropped-die marking, labeled
  rolls ("Attack (Spear)" + auto damage), Shift+Enter advantage/disadvantage menu,
  correct math throughout, ARIA labels on results, **live bidirectional broadcast**.
- **Character creation wizard structure**: 8-step stepper with disabled-until-valid
  gating, info-rich species/class cards, ASI duplicate-prevention, Standard Array that
  consumes values as assigned, live derived stats. (Add the spells step and it's the
  best-in-class flow it almost is.)
- **Sheet rules math**: HP/AC/saves/skills/weapon attack+damage/weapon mastery
  (Sap/Vex with tooltips) all correct per 5.5E across both builds.
- **Spell-slot mechanics**: slot pips, upcast dialog, auto-disable at 0 slots,
  cantrips always-prepared, Long Rest preview listing exactly what restores.
- **Battle map**: real 30×30 grid VTT — all 5E AoE templates, fog of war, measure,
  drawing layer, tokens, zoom/fit, unified game log with chat.
- **Campaign IA**: deep-linkable tabs, good empty states, NPC visibility model
  (hidden-by-default + separate DM notes), invite flow accepting code *or* link with
  friendly 409 handling, correct player/DM tab scoping.
- **Mobile (390×844)**: hamburger nav, fully usable chat, and the sheet smartly
  reflows into Stats/Combat/Features/Gear/Spells/Info tabs.
- **Live sync where wired**: chat, dice, presence, damage-HP, encounter status
  transitions all broadcast reliably — which is exactly why the unwired events
  (turn pointer, roster, sheet-side heals) feel broken by contrast.

## Suggested fix order

1. P0-1 spell selection (blocks every new caster; self-contained wizard work).
2. P0-2 + P1-8 + P2-18 socket/cache-invalidation sweep (turn pointer, roster
   assignment, heal/condition events) — one coherent realtime workstream; see
   `docs/socket-architecture.md` + `docs/guides/add-socket-broadcast.md`.
3. P0-3 HP ownership decision (snapshot vs single-source + event surfacing) — needs a
   short design note first; touches `docs/CONCURRENCY.md` territory.
4. P1-4 AC derivation (small, high trust impact).
5. P1-5 rest-modal close (one-liner-ish, blocker-feeling bug).
6. P1-9 a11y sweep (mechanical: DialogDescription + aria-pressed).
7. The P1-6/P1-7 action-surface work is the biggest *product* opportunity — turn the
   three-surface combat dance into one flow; worth its own design pass rather than a
   quick fix.
